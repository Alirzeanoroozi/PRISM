# Mission: Trustworthy PRISM provenance

## Why

I want to understand what a provenance manifest can and cannot prove so I can design and interpret PRISM docking runs without confusing operational completion with scientific correctness.

## Success looks like

- Distinguish a declared contract, an execution attempt, an artifact observation, and run closure.
- Inspect a manifest and state exactly which claims are supported and which remain unproven.
- Identify the smallest additional evidence needed before trusting a comparison or DockQ result.

## Constraints

- Lessons should stay short, concrete, and tied to the current PRISM repository.
- Prefer retrieval and small exercises over passive explanation.
- Use primary technical sources and existing project evidence.

## Out of scope

- Implementing the Phase 1 provenance system during lessons.
- Building a complete W3C PROV graph or covering later evaluation/HPC phases before the foundation is understood.
